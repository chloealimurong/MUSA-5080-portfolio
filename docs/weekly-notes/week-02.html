census data

Decennial Census

  • 10 years
  • 10 questions
  • long form

American Community Survey (ACS)

  • 2005
  • 3% of households are surveyed on income, commute, ethnicity, rent, etc..
  • Results in an estimate for the population
  • 1-year ACS conducted in places > 65,000 people (data available and current at metropolitan level)
  • 5-year ACS (block groups and census tracts)
    • each year represents a 5-year average
    • estimate +- a margin of error (MOE)

B1234_001E

B is the Base table

1234 is the table number

001E is the Line number

E is the Estimate

M is the MOE

load_variables()

state → county → tract (neighborhood of 5-8,000 people and boundaries change frequently)

GEOID

42101000100

42 is the state

101 is the county

000100 is the tract (11 digits)

block groups is the 12th

  • always stored as string
  • make sure u check the way its stored before trying to do anything with it

## verbs

- filter(): picks rows
- select(): picks columns
- mutate(): add a new computed column
- arrange():  change the order of rows (asc, desc)
- group_by() + summarize(): many rows become few

Step 1.

filter(counties, moe_pct>10)

Step 2.

arrange(Step1, desc(moe_pct))

Step 3.

select(NAME, moe_pct)

- Want to write “and then”?
    - %>% or |> : strings functions without saving an intermediate file along the way like steps 1-3.

Steps 1-3 become:

```jsx
new_data ← counties %>%
    filter(moe_pct) %>%
    arrange(desc(moe_pct))%>%
    select(NAME, moe_pct)
```
