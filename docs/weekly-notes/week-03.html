ACS does not have MOE

  • Coefficient of Variation (cv): relative measure of sampling error

(standard error/ estimate) * 100

  • standard error (90%): moe/1.65

  • encompasses how many people u ask and how varied that sample is

  • low cv threshold: 12% vs census 15% (high reliability)

  • Derived Uncertainty:

    pov_rate = (# in pov/total pop) +- moe

moe_sum() → adds estimates together

moe_prop() → one estimate as share of another

moe_ratio() → ratio of two different variables that are not subset of another

∆ over time

  1. Overlapping Intervals
    1. e.g. 5-yr estimates that overlap (2019-2023 & 2018-2022)
    2. you have to compare the non-overlapping intervals 2014-2018 vs 2019-2023
  2. The Standard Error of Difference
    1. can the difference actually have been zero ? since they are just samples, can’t tell based off estimate alone (hypothesis.)
    2. SEdiff = (SE1^2 + SE^2)
    3. z = (est1-est2)/SEdiff
    4. 1.645
  3. Confidence Interval of Change
    1. Difference +- (1.645*SEdiff)
